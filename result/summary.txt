**IV. Key Definitions & Terminology**

*   **Rate of Change:** The measure of how much a function's output changes in relation to changes in its input.
*   **Slope:**  Represents the rate of change of a linear function.
*   **Secant Line:** A line passing through two points on a curve, used to approximate the tangent line.
*   **Tangent Line:** The line that "touches" a curve at a single point and represents the instantaneous rate of change at that point.
*   **Derivative:**  The slope of the tangent line; represents the instantaneous rate of change.
*   **Average Velocity:** Change in position divided by time elapsed.
*   **Instantaneous Velocity:** The limit of average velocities as the time interval approaches zero.
